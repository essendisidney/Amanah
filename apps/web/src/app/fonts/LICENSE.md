# Fonts

Latin subsets of two variable fonts, downloaded from Google Fonts and served by the app
through `next/font/local` (see `../layout.tsx`).

- `manrope-latin.woff2`: Manrope by Mikhail Sharanda, https://github.com/sharanda/manrope
- `space-grotesk-latin.woff2`: Space Grotesk by Florian Karsten, https://github.com/floriankarsten/space-grotesk

Both are licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1: https://openfontlicense.org
